# Removing mods

Saves are never edited. When you remove a mod:

- Its items stay in your inventory, but equipped ones are lost the next time the game saves.
- Its buildings disappear and are lost the next time the game saves.

Mods with `"preserve": true` keep track of their buildings in each save. If you install such a mod again, its buildings come back where they were, with the items they held, except ones you took down yourself. The record stays in saves made while the mod was removed, as long as the mod manager runs the game.
